## English

### New

- **Lower the sea floor** (heightmap dialog, section "Water and shores", on by default): The height data contain no sea depths, so the sea used to lie flat at 0 m and fell dry as a beach in the game. It now lies 2 m below the water level at the coast and is lowered smoothly towards the set depth (default 10 m) further out. Thanks to MattiZockt1234 for the report.
- **Build template as image** (button in the heightmap dialog): All roads coloured by class, tracks in black and white blocks in trackmap style and platforms in grey, saved as `.tga` and `.png` the size of the map on the heightmap grid. A template for rebuilding 1:1; nothing is built in the game. Thanks to Th3_d4rk_sh4d0w for the request.
